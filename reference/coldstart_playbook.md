# Cold Start & Zero-to-One Playbook

> Compiled by parallel deep reading from 3,529 real founder interviews on "launch / go-live / first customer"
> General ranking: cross-shard occurrence frequency = generality score

---

## 1. Cold Start Paths (12 paths, ranked by generality)

| # | Path | Generality | Core Actions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Audience-first (build audience before launching)** | 8/8 | Produce content / build an email list for months before launch; convert the warm list directly at launch. |
| 2 | **Pre-sell to validate before production** | 8/8 | Collect money / emails with a landing page first; develop or produce only after paid commitment. |
| 3 | **Community penetration (give value first)** | 8/8 | Sincerely answer questions hundreds of times on Reddit / Quora / FB groups, then casually mention your product. |
| 4 | **Warm network / warm introductions** | 7/8 | Get the first deal from friends / former colleagues / former clients; exchange exceptional service for referrals. |
| 5 | **Cold outreach / manual sales** | 7/8 | Precise target list + personalized messaging; close one by one. |
| 6 | **SEO content compounding** | 7/8 | Pick low-competition long-tail keywords, produce consistently, build stable acquisition over months. |
| 7 | **Free for seed users (free-to-paid)** | 6/8 | Give it away free for downloads / reviews / feedback; add a paywall only when users start asking to pay. |
| 8 | **Platform-first (Etsy / Amazon / App Store)** | 6/8 | List minimally on an established platform to borrow traffic; validate before building your own site. |
| 9 | **Product Hunt / directory launches** | 6/8 | Polish assets and aim for the leaderboard; trade rank for seed users and SEO backlinks. |
| 10 | **Crowdfunding (Kickstarter)** | 6/8 | Crowdfunding validates the market + raises seed capital + gets first customers at once. |
| 11 | **Build in Public + founder brand** | 5/8 | Continuously show the building process, accumulate an audience, convert on launch day. |
| 12 | **Offline events / fairs / tastings** | 5/8 | Go where customers gather offline; demo, sample, and close deals in person. |

## 2. Where the First Customer Comes From (9 types)

| # | Source | Generality |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Friends & family / personal network / warm introductions | 8/8 |
| 2 | Cold outreach / cold DMs | 7/8 |
| 3 | Free work / free trials in exchange for word of mouth | 7/8 |
| 4 | Community posts / replies | 7/8 |
| 5 | Content / SEO passive acquisition | 6/8 |
| 6 | Pre-sale / waitlist | 5/8 |
| 7 | First anonymous order on a platform | 5/8 |
| 8 | Influencers / affiliates | 5/8 |
| 9 | Anchor customer endorsement | 4/8 |

**Meta-law**: Most first customers come from **"existing trust relationships"** (friends & family, warm intros, past clients) or **"trade value for trust first"** (free work, community Q&A, content). Pure cold start (cold outreach) also works but ranks after trust-first.

## 3. Counterintuitive Cold Start Lessons

| # | Lesson | Generality |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Don't chase perfection; ship early** (if you're not embarrassed at launch, you shipped too late) | 8/8 |
| 2 | **Built ≠ discovered** (nobody will magically find you) | 7/8 |
| 3 | **Get paid first / sell before building** | 6/8 |
| 4 | **Do unscalable things** (one-by-one calls, manual onboarding) | 6/8 |
| 5 | **Ads often fail; organic wins** (validate before spending) | 6/8 |
| 6 | **Focus on 1-2 channels**, don't spread out | 5/8 |
| 7 | **Friends & family promises rarely come true** (liking ≠ paying) | 4/8 |
| 8 | **Audience first, product second** | 4/8 |
| 9 | **Don't chase the first deal's amount; you want the "paying" action itself** | 4/8 |
| 10 | **The founder should be the marketer/salesperson**; outsourcing to "experts" often yields zero | 4/8 |

**Key judgments (the skill's cold start formula)**:
1. **Trust > traffic**: the first customer comes from a trust relationship or value exchange, not ads.
2. **The paying action > the amount paid**: going zero-to-one, the psychological barrier of the action is harder to cross than the dollar amount.
3. **Manual > automated (early)**: unscalable manual actions earn feedback and word of mouth; scaling comes after you have traction.
